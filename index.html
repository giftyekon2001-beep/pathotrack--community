Health Tips

🧼

Wash Hands Regularly

Wash your hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, especially before eating and after using the bathroom.

💧

Drink Clean Water

Always drink clean, filtered, or boiled water to prevent waterborne infections and diseases.

⚠️

Avoid Self-Medication

Do not self-medicate. Consult a healthcare professional before taking any medications.

🍎

Practice Food Hygiene

Store food properly, cook at appropriate temperatures, and avoid eating expired or contaminated food.

😷

Maintain Hygiene

Cover your mouth and nose when coughing or sneezing to prevent the spread of infections.

🏥

Seek Medical Help

If symptoms persist or worsen, seek medical help from a qualified healthcare provider immediately.
